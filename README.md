# Canadian Freelancer Tax Resources

A practical guide for Canadian freelancers, contractors, and sole proprietors 
filing taxes with the CRA.

## T2125 - Statement of Business Activities

If you are self-employed in Canada, you file a T2125 as part of your personal 
T1 return. This covers:

- Business income and expenses
- Vehicle deduction using the actual expense method
- Home office deduction
- Capital cost allowance

## HST Registration Thresholds

CRA requires you to register for HST when:

- Your taxable revenue exceeds $30,000 in a single calendar quarter, OR
- Your taxable revenue exceeds $30,000 across four consecutive calendar quarters

You have 29 days to register once you cross either threshold.

**Exception:** Taxi and rideshare drivers must register from their very first fare.

## KM Logbook Requirements

CRA requires a contemporaneous logbook for vehicle deductions. Each entry must include:

- Date of trip
- Destination (from and to)
- Business purpose
- Kilometres driven
- Odometer readings (recommended)

The logbook must be kept as you go - reconstructing it at year end is not accepted.

## T2125 Vehicle Deduction Method

Self-employed Canadians use the **actual expense method**:

Business use % = Business KM / Total KM (from odometer)

Claimable amount = Business use % x Total vehicle costs

Total vehicle costs include:
- Insurance
- Fuel
- Maintenance and repairs
- Loan interest (to CRA limit)
- Registration fees
- Leasing costs (to CRA limit)

**Note:** The 73 cents per kilometre rate is the CRA employee allowance. 
It does not apply to self-employed T2125 filers.

## Home Office Deduction

You can claim home office expenses if you meet one of:

- Your home is your principal place of business, OR
- You use the space exclusively and regularly to meet clients

Claimable expenses include rent, mortgage interest, utilities, internet, 
and home insurance at your business use percentage.

## HST Input Tax Credits (ITCs)

When you are registered for HST, you can claim back the HST you paid on 
business expenses. This is called an Input Tax Credit (ITC).

HST owing = HST collected on income - ITCs (HST paid on expenses)

## CRA Record Retention

CRA requires you to keep all business records for **6 years** from the end 
of the tax year they relate to.
